Frequently Asked Questions

What is your turnaround time?

For standard blog posts (1,000 words), my turnaround is typically 3-5 business days. Larger projects like website copy overhauls take 2-3 weeks depending on scope.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! Every single project I quote includes up to two rounds of revisions. My goal is to ensure you are 100% satisfied with the final copy.

Do you write in American or British English?

I am highly proficient in both US and UK English conventions. I will adapt my spelling and grammatical style to match your specific target audience.
