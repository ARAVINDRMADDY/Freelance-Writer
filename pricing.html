Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, revisions, and how we collaborate.

Do you require a deposit?
Yes, I require a 50% deposit upfront to secure your spot in my calendar. The remaining 50% is due upon project completion and your final sign-off.
What happens if I need revisions?
Almost all my packages (unless stated otherwise) include up to two rounds of minor revisions within 14 days of the first draft delivery to ensure you are 100% happy with the tone and message.
Do you offer rush delivery?
Standard turnaround times are typically 5-7 business days. If you need something within 48 hours, a 30% rush fee will be applied to the total project cost, subject to my availability.
How do you handle payments?
Invoices are sent via secure software and can be paid using all major Credit Cards, PayPal, or direct Bank Transfer (ACH/Wire).

My Quality Guarantee

I am committed to delivering copy that sounds like you and sells like crazy. If the first draft doesn't hit the mark, we will work together through revisions until it aligns perfectly with your brief. Your success is my success.
